Gummy Sudoku

Mini 4×4 · Easy

How to play

  • Every row needs one of each gummy
  • Every column tooTop to bottom works just like a row.
  • And every boxBoxes are the squares with thick edges.
  • No doubles!

Tap a square, then a gummy to fill it. Or pick a gummy first, then tap squares.

Not sure? Tap Maybe to jot a small reminder in a square's corner.

Stuck? Try Hint, or turn on Show where it fits in settings.

Gummies with a small dot were there from the start.
